The deck standard for this course

Eight rules that apply to my decks and to your project decks

The four SIAP sentences are written before the slide software is opened
Every slide title states a finding, not a topic label
One message per slide — two points means two slides
The conclusion comes first, the evidence follows (answer-first)
One accent colour per chart, everything else stays grey
Figures are rounded to the unit the decision is made in
Source and period appear on the slide
It closes with a request that has an owner and a deadline
