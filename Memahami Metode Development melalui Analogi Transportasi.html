Metode 1 · Waterfall

Waterfall menyelesaikan tahap demi tahap; pengguna baru bisa berangkat di akhir

Kelebihan

Rencana, anggaran, dan jadwal jelas sejak awal; dokumentasi rapi dan mudah diaudit.

Kekurangan

Umpan balik pengguna datang di akhir; perubahan setelah tahap dikunci mahal.

Cocok bila

Kebutuhan stabil dan diatur ketat: payroll sesuai aturan pajak, migrasi data, konstruksi.

S1 · PPMPlanning tuntas di awal; Control mudah: bandingkan rencana dengan realisasi di setiap gerbang tahap.
S2 · ABAsumsi kebutuhan baru teruji setelah sebagian besar anggaran terpakai.

Metode 3 · Incremental versus Agile

Incremental menambah bagian sesuai rencana; Agile menambah pembelajaran

Incremental · pola membangun
Yang ditambahBagian atau fitur yang sudah direncanakan
Arah ditentukanRencana awal
Contoh softwareERP dirilis per modul: pembelian, gudang, keuangan
Pertanyaan kuncinya“Bagian mana berikutnya, sesuai rencana?”
Agile · cara bekerja
Yang ditambahPembelajaran dari pengguna
Arah ditentukanUmpan balik terbaru
Contoh softwareAplikasi pesan-antar: fitur berikutnya dipilih dari data
Pertanyaan kuncinya“Apa yang kita pelajari, dan apa yang perlu diubah?”
Ujian sederhana: bisakah isi tahap berikutnya berubah karena umpan balik? Jika tidak, itu incremental, belum Agile.

Method 1 · Waterfall

Waterfall finishes one stage after another; users only set off at the end

Strengths

Plan, budget and schedule are clear from day one; documents are tidy and auditable.

Weaknesses

User feedback comes at the end; change after a stage is locked is costly.

Fits when

Needs are stable and tightly regulated: payroll under tax rules, data migration, construction.

S1 · PPMPlanning is done up front; Control is easy: compare plan with actual at every stage gate.
S2 · ABAssumptions about needs are tested only after most of the budget is spent.

Method 3 · Incremental versus Agile

Incremental adds parts to a plan; Agile adds learning

Incremental · a way of building
What growsParts or features already planned
Direction set byThe original plan
Software exampleERP released per module: purchasing, warehouse, finance
The key question“Which part comes next, as planned?”
Agile · a way of working
What growsLearning from users
Direction set byThe latest feedback
Software exampleDelivery app: next feature chosen from usage data
The key question“What did we learn, and what should change?”
A simple test: can the next stage change because of feedback? If not, it is incremental, not yet Agile.
Incremental vs Agile008 / 020

Method 4 · Prototype

A prototype tests the idea with a cheap model before the big investment

Kinds of prototype
  • Paper sketches or wireframes
  • Clickable mock-ups (e.g. Figma)
  • “Wizard of Oz”: the service is run by hand behind the scenes
Where the analogy stops

Nobody will drive a cardboard car. A software prototype often looks finished, gets pushed into release, and its technical problems come along.

S1 · PPMUnderstand is tested cheaply: go, change or stop is decided with a small team and little money.
S2 · ABA prototype is a hypothesis test: set the pass criteria before testing, not after.
Prototype009 / 020

Approach 5 · AI-assisted development

AI speeds up exploring and building, but it is not a method and it does not decide

Where AI sits · a support layer
Waterfall
Incremental
Agile
Prototype
AI-assisted · can sit inside any method

The method still sets the order of work. AI speeds up the steps inside it: draft requirements, design options, code, tests and feedback summaries.

AI's limits
  • Can be wrong very convincingly
  • Carries bias from its training data
  • Confidential data can leak if uploaded carelessly
  • Accountability stays with people and the organisation
S1 · PPMOrganizing shifts: who uses AI, who checks its output, who signs off (Control).
S2 · ABIt needs governance: data quality, bias testing, an audit trail and a human in the loop.

Separate project KPIs (on time) from outcome KPIs (conversion, retention).

Experiments

A small release can be designed as an A/B test with a control group.

Hypothesis testing

“Personal recommendations lift conversion”: test it, do not assume it.

Monitoring

Watch performance, data drift and complaints after release.

Decisions · uncertainty

Small reversible steps are worth more when uncertainty is high.

Model / product evaluation

Offline accuracy is not business impact; test online too.

AI governance & oversight

Name the data owner, the model approver and when a human must review.

Short cycles only pay off if the hypothesis, metric and decision rule are written before release.
